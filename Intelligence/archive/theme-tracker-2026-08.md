| Week in cyclus | Hoofdthema | Subonderwerpen |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Wet DBA / ZZP | Schijnzelfstandigheid, modelovereenkomst, vrije vervanging, gezagsverhouding, opdrachtovereenkomst |
| Week 2 | AVG / Privacy | Verwerkingsregister, datalekprotocol, DPA, cookieverklaring, AP-boetes, DPIA |
| Week 3 | HR / Arbeidscontracten | Concurrentiebeding, ontslag, vaststellingsovereenkomst, verbeterplan, addendum, aanzegbrief |
| Week 4 | Product + social proof | Feature highlights, document-clusters, use cases, AI Document Review, testimonials |
